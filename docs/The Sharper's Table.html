How to Play

The Deal

Everyone antes 5, then each player is dealt five cards face down.

First Betting Round

Starting left of the dealer, players check, bet, call, or fold. Betting is fixed-limit, capped at three raises per round.

The Draw

Each remaining player may discard any number of cards and receive that many replacements from the deck.

Second Betting Round & Showdown

A second betting round follows the draw. Players still in reveal their hands, and the best standard poker hand (High Card through Straight Flush) takes the pot.

The House Wins

Your chips are spent.
